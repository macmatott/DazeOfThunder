Sim Racing Scoring

Every classified finisher scores under our own NASCAR-inspired scale — no cutoff at 10th like real F1. The win is worth the full grid size, then points drop by one per position, down to 1 point for last.

Real F1 pays a sprint at roughly a third of a full race, so ours does too — the full race scale, thirded and rounded, with the win bumped a point above 2nd so they don't tie. Still every finisher scores, same as the race.

Same NASCAR-inspired shape, scaled to our own 11-person grid instead of F1's — every finisher scores, the win is worth 11 points, and points drop by one per position after a small bonus over 2nd place.
